# Link prospects (SEO stage 13, researched 2026-09-26)

Pages that already link out to other sites' Bible, Advent and homeschool resources, where the owner could ask for a
link by hand. Each page was loaded and checked for outbound resource links; nothing was submitted or contacted.
The owner decided (2026-09-26): no email marketing, so outreach is by hand, one site at a time, when the owner
chooses. Contact paths are only the ones the sites publish; check the footer where it says "not verified".

Our link targets: `/tags/advent` (the 25-day Advent coloring calendar), `/tags/christmas`, `/coloring-pages` and
its story pages, `/bible-verse-coloring` (the verse generator).
