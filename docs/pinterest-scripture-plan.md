# Scripture board plan (verse art)

Researched 2026-09-23 in Pinterest Trends (signed-in owner account; US searches, 52 weeks to 2026-09-20). Part of
[pinterest-strategy.md](pinterest-strategy.md); the copy rules there (§3.3a) still apply.

## 1. What people search

**How it was measured.** Trends' prefix search surfaced ~95 passages that clear Pinterest's volume threshold. Each
was compared with `philippians 4 13` (= 100) in the same query, which puts every term on one scale. Pinterest splits a
passage across spellings and add-ons ("jeremiah 29 11", "jeremiah 29 11 tattoo", "for i know the plans i have for
you"), so the demand below is the sum of a passage's variants. Tattoo and wallpaper variants are not coloring intent,
but they show which verses people want to see beautifully lettered, which is what verse art is.

**The key finding:** people search the **passage**, not "coloring page". `philippians 4 13` alone is 100;
`bible verse coloring pages` is 16, `scripture coloring sheets` 7, `christian coloring pages` 23. Our verse Pins get
found through passage searches, so the reference must lead the title.

## 2. What to produce

**The Scripture board is for adults, mostly women** (owner decision 2026-09-23). 73% of our audience are women,
every verse Pin that worked was adult-styled, and the big verse searches are adult ("aesthetic", tattoo, wallpaper,
"Proverbs 31 woman", friendship). Kids verse searches are small and seasonal, and a kids page would sit in a
different interest cluster (strategy §1.2): it belongs on the Sunday School board.

**Two looks per Tier 1 passage**, each a different sketch (a new image to the same passage keeps ~64% of reach,
strategy §1.10), never the same art in another banner. The looks are adult styles:
- **S, script and botanicals**: Elegant Script or Classic Serif lettering, flowers and leaves, lots of white. The
  default; serves the "aesthetic", "wallpaper" and "Proverbs 31 woman" demand.
- **M, bold modern**: big modern or brush lettering, few ornaments; the tattoo and wallpaper look.
- **D, doodle**: hand-drawn doodles around the verse ("scripture doodles" peaks Feb and Apr); an adult trend.

**Kids memory verses** are a small test on the **Sunday School board**, not here: back to school (late July -
August) and before Easter. The first one is the playful Joshua 1:9 page on Sept 30. They get more slots only if
their saves match the story scenes.

Tier 2: one look each, the one that fits the verse. Tier 3: only in its season.

**Translation.** The verse-art pipeline uses the World English Bible, which prints God's name as "Yahweh" ("Yahweh
is my shepherd"). The verses people search and save are worded "the LORD" (our old NIV-worded Joshua 1:9 Pin has
385 saves). The same free Bible API offers public-domain alternatives: WEB British Edition (`webbe`: "The LORD is my
shepherd; I shall lack nothing", but British spelling) and KJV ("The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want"). NIV and
ESV are what most people quote but are copyrighted. **Owner decision (2026-09-23): keep WEB, print "the LORD".**
`withLord()` in `functions/generation/pipeline.js` swaps the name after the fetch ("Yahweh is my shepherd" → "The LORD is
my shepherd", "O Yahweh" → "O LORD", "Lord Yahweh" → "Lord GOD"; check: `node scripts/check-verse-name.mjs`). KJV
was considered and dropped: archaic, and its Jeremiah 29:11 reads "the thoughts that I think toward you". Verse art
prints only the **first verse** of a range, so ranges in the calendar are picked by their strongest single verse.

## 3. Copy for scripture Pins

The reference is what people type, and only the first 35-45 characters show, so scripture titles lead with it:

`[Reference] Coloring Page: [first words of the verse] | [audience] Bible Verse`
- `Joshua 1:9 Coloring Page: Be Strong and Courageous | Bible Journaling Verse`
- `Proverbs 31:25 Coloring Page: She Is Clothed with Strength | Aesthetic Bible Verse`

Audience words that fit: Aesthetic Bible Verse, Bible Journaling, Scripture for Women, Quiet Time. It keeps every checked rule (contains "Coloring" and "|", no "Free"). Descriptions quote the verse in full: the verse
words are searched too ("for i know the plans i have for you" alone is 21 on this scale).

## 5. How we'll know

At day 30 and 90 (strategy §4), per Scripture Pin: save rate and outbound CTR, compared by
- Tier (does search demand predict saves on our Pins?),
- look (adult aesthetic vs kids memory verse),
- translation, once a KJV/WEBBE batch exists (vs the WEB "Yahweh" pages).

Later, not now: Spanish verse art (28% of our audience is in Latin America; "Salmo 23", "Filipenses 4:13") needs
Spanish pages on the site first; verse-mapping or scripture-writing printables are a product idea, not a Pin.
